【사진 01】 shots/01-battle.png

오늘은 방치형 밸런스 얻어터졌던 얘기 좀 하려고 합니다

문제가 뭐였느냐면, 적 HP는 지수로 크는데 플레이어 파워는 그만큼 안 큽니다.
업그레이드 비용이 1.3^n으로 오르는데 효과는 1.05^n이면 DPS가 골드의 0.18승
수준으로밖에 안 자랍니다. 지수 vs 분수승이니까 언젠가는 반드시 벌어집니다

실제로 제 초기 버전이 그랬습니다. 시뮬 돌려보니:
- 42지방까지는 보스가 그냥 즉사
- 43지방 3일 → 44지방 9일 → 45지방 21일 → 46지방 53일
- 그 뒤로는 계산기 터짐

42지방까지 즉사하다가 5지방 만에 백일단위로 가는 겁니다.
완만한 감속이 아니라 그냥 벽입니다

계수 하나 만지작거리는 걸로는 안 되더군요. 벽을 약하게 하면 이번엔 반나절에
끝나버리고(50지방을 반나절에 뚫음), 세게 하면 다시 절벽. 그 사이 구간이
칼날처럼 좁아서 튜닝으로는 못 잡습니다

결국 구조를 바꿨습니다:
1. 모든 비용·보상을 '지방 배수' 하나에 앵커 → 비율이 영원히 안 깨짐(발산 불가)

**Title:** `I made an idle Pokémon auto-battler that docks in the corner of your desktop (free, Win/Mac)`

```markdown
Solo dev, hobby project. I like Pokémon and I like those tiny "docked at the edge of
your screen" idle games, so I made one for myself. A few friends played it and the ones
into Pokémon actually stuck with it, so I figured I'd put it out and see what people
here think. It's free, no monetization of any kind — I'm mostly after feedback.

**Download (free, Windows + macOS):** https://github.com/lie-0022/pokemon-battle-room/releases/latest

【photo 01】 shots/01-battle.png

A small window sits in a corner of your desktop while you do other things. A party of 3
auto-battles through waves -> route boss -> regional champion -> next region, endlessly.
But it's not just a progress bar going up — there's a real amount to tune.

## Moves, stats, IVs, shinies

【photo 03】 shots/03-member-stats.png

- All 151 Gen 1 species with actual base stats (6 stats) from PokeAPI. Physical moves use Atk vs Def, special moves use SpA vs SpD
- Full 18-type matchup chart. In battle each Pokemon auto-picks whichever of its 4 moves is most effective against the current enemy
- You set 4 active moves from everything it has learned (level-up + TMs). Power and accuracy are real values
- Setup moves like Dragon Dance and Swords Dance are only available to species that actually learn them, and they get used automatically in boss fights
- IVs 0-31 — same species at the same level still differ. Max one out and it gets a crown
- Shinies at 1% from the wild (the ones in the shot are all shiny)

## Gear — 3 slots per Pokemon

【photo 04】 shots/04-gear.png

- Choice Band, Life Orb, Assault Vest, Eviolite, Scope Lens, type plates, etc. — all with distinct roles
- Star ranks, fuse 3 identical items to push one to the next star (fail = lose one)
- Fusion odds are based on how far above your *current progress* you're pushing, not on absolute star level, so the practical ceiling rises as you advance instead of hard-walling
- Auto-equip reads each Pokemon's physical/special bias and typing and picks for you

## Collection

【photo 06】 shots/06-dex.png

- 151-entry Pokedex with mastery at 100 kills each, milestone rewards along the way
- Online leaderboards for stage progress and raid speedruns — you can open other players' team setups

## Raids — 10-Pokemon teams

【photo 07】 shots/07-raid.png

- Match the boss's weaknesses or your damage falls off a cliff. There's a per-hit damage cap, so you can't just overlevel through it
- Stacking one type gives a synergy bonus (+15% at 3, +30% at 5)
- Tiers are banded (Normal -> Elite -> Legend -> Hell, then endless), each with a different boss

Also: daily missions with streaks, offline gold, 1-3x battle speed, save export/import.

## Notes

- Free, no ads, no monetization, no accounts. Non-commercial fan project.
- macOS is unsigned, so you need `xattr -cr /Applications/PokemonBattleRoom.app` once.
- English support is fairly new. If you hit an awkward phrase or an untranslated string, tell me and I'll patch it.

## Something I'd like feedback on

The idle curve took a full redesign. My first version looked fine for ~40 regions then
hit a brick wall: enemy HP grows geometrically, but player DPS from upgrades only grows
as a fractional power of gold spent, so clear times went from 3 days to 50+ days across
about five regions. I fixed it by anchoring every cost and reward to a single region
multiplier and making bosses pure HP gates rather than DPS checks.

If you push deep and it still walls somewhere, I'd really like to know where.
```
